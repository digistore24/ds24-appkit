## Two different things

**A plan** is a right. While it runs, the features it covers are open to you.
It is billed on a schedule — monthly or yearly — and it ends when it is
cancelled or when the paid period runs out.

**Credit** is a quantity. You buy a package, the balance goes up, and using
certain features takes some of it away. Credit does not expire on a schedule; it
is spent.

An app may sell one, the other or both. What you see on the plans page is what
this one sells.

## Buying

The plans page lists what is available. Checkout runs at Digistore24, the
platform we sell through, and you can pay without being signed in first — the
purchase attaches to your account the next time you sign in with the same email
address.

## Invoices, payment details and cancelling

*Billing* in the menu. Every purchase is listed there, and for a running
subscription you will find the links to view the invoice, change your payment
details and cancel.

Cancelling stops the next payment. It does **not** cut your access short: you
keep what you paid for until the end of the period you already paid for.

## When a payment fails

A missed payment pauses your access rather than ending it. Update your payment
details through the *Billing* page and it comes back on the next successful
charge. You will see a note saying your access is paused, not that your account
is gone.

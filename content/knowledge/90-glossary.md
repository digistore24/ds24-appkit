## Plan

A right to use certain features, running for as long as it is paid for. Also
called a subscription when it renews on a schedule.

## Credit

Prepaid quantity, counted in whole units, spent by using a feature that costs
something. Sometimes called tokens. A balance is not a plan: having credit does
not unlock a plan's features, and having a plan does not top up credit.

## Access

What you may use right now. It comes from your purchases and, occasionally, from
something support granted you by hand. It is not the same as your payment
history: a cancelled subscription still gives access until the period you paid
for ends.

## Paused access

What a missed payment produces. The features are closed, the account is intact,
and a successful payment opens them again.

## Order

One payment. It carries the order number in your confirmation email, and it is
what support asks for when something about a purchase needs looking up.

## Digistore24

The platform this app sells through. Checkout, invoices, payment details and
cancellation all happen there, which is why some links lead off this site.

## Auto top-up

An optional setting on prepaid credit: when your balance falls below a
threshold, the same package you bought before is charged again automatically.
It is off unless you switched it on at checkout.

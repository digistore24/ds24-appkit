## The steps

1. Open *Billing* from the menu on the left.
2. Find the running subscription in the list.
3. Follow the **cancel** link. It takes you to Digistore24, the platform we
   sell through, where the cancellation is confirmed.

## What happens then

- The next payment does not happen.
- **Your access continues to the end of the period you have already paid for.**
  Cancelling on day two of a month you paid for does not cost you the other
  twenty-eight.
- You keep any prepaid credit on your balance. Credit is bought separately and
  is not part of the subscription.

## What cancelling does not do

It does not delete your account, and it does not remove your data. If that is
what you want, contact support and say so — it is a separate request, and some
records (invoices, for example) have to be kept for a legally fixed period even
then.

## Changing plan instead

If you want a different plan rather than none, buy the new one — the old
subscription's billing is stopped as part of the switch. For a day or two around
the change you may see both plans listed. That is normal and nothing is being
charged twice.
